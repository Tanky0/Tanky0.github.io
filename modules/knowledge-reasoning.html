Learning Outcomes

On completion of this module, you will be able to:

  1. Critique the need for formal approaches to knowledge representation and reasoning.
  2. Review critically properties of a knowledge-based system.
  3. Appraise critically modelling techniques for knowledge representation and reasoning.
  4. Examine and incorporate different modelling approaches to solving KRR problems.

Skills to be Gained

  • Time management
  • Critical thinking and analysis
  • Communication and literacy skills
  • IT and digital
  • Numeracy
  • Research
  • Interpersonal
  • Problem-solving
  • Ethical awareness

Assignments

The e-portfolio for this module is expected to include:

  • Collaborative Discussion Forum Summaries – covering learning outcomes 1 to 3.
  • Reflective Piece – covering all learning outcomes, as well as the module aims.
