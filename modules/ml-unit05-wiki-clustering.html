Findings

Findings
ActivityWhat I didResult
Animation 1 (Shabalin, no date)All four centres started at the leftmost pointsOne group split and two merged: a local optimum, a stable answer that is not the best one (Géron, 2025).
Animation 2 (Harris, 2014)Ten centres bunched in the middle of evenly spread pointsK-means still produced ten tidy regions; restarts cannot fix this.
Python check (250 points, 3 samples)SSE and silhouette for k = 1 to 8, on even points and on four simulated groupsEven points: silhouette 0.36 to 0.42 for every k. Four groups: 0.76 to 0.78 at k = 4.

K-means almost always returns the number of groups it is asked for, so a tidy result is not proof that the groups exist. The elbow plot alone was not enough: the even data seemed to bend at four, while the silhouette stayed near 0.4. If an insurer made one offer per cluster, near-identical customers on either side of an invented boundary could get different deals (University of Essex Online, 2026).
