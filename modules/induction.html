Module Overview

The Induction module serves as the foundational entry point to the MSc Artificial Intelligence programme, providing essential context for postgraduate study in computing. This module introduces the academic expectations, key concepts, and professional standards that underpin the entire programme, whilst establishing the framework for critical engagement with AI as both a technical discipline and a sociotechnological phenomenon.

Learning Outcomes

Through this module, I developed understanding of the importance of postgraduate study in artificial intelligence, the role of computing professionals in contemporary society, and the expectations for academic rigour and professional conduct throughout the programme. The module emphasised the significance of bridging practical experience with formal computing education, particularly for professionals transitioning from industry contexts into academic study.

Assignment

Induction Essay: The Importance of a Postgraduate Degree in the Field of Artificial Intelligence

Brief Description: A 750-word essay examining the significance of postgraduate study in artificial intelligence, relating the topic to my specific field and programme. The essay required clear introduction of my field of study and degree type, explanation of the importance of both field and degree, reflection on personal experience and observations, and consideration of how undertaking this degree might affect the field and society at large, including expectations from completing the course.
