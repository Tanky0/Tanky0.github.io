This ePortfolio documents my learning journey through the MSc in Artificial Intelligence programme at the University of Essex. It serves as a comprehensive collection of artefacts, reflections, and evidence of skill development across all programme modules.

The portfolio demonstrates my understanding of module content and its application in wider social and global contexts, my ability to draw connections between module material and coursework to achieve learning objectives, and my capacity for self-assessment, reflecting on personal strengths and weaknesses whilst identifying achievements with supporting evidence.
