About Me

My name is Anne Dayer, AI Governance Lead for the global Central Laboratory and Bioanalytical services of Labcorp, a US-based life sciences company with a global footprint. Based in Switzerland with over twenty years navigating the intersection of life sciences, data strategy, and innovation, current responsibilities encompass AI strategy, governance, and portfolio oversight. This includes identifying and selecting appropriate AI projects, establishing operating models, driving adoption of AI tools by staff and clients, and developing general AI literacy across the organisation. Pursuing an MSc in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Essex builds the technical depth needed to bridge strategic vision with responsible implementation.

A single, current assessment of my professional skills across the programme, with the personal development plan for each skill held in the final column. It is updated as each module completes; the version below was submitted as Appendix A of the Numerical Analysis End of Module Assessment 2 reflection and awarded full marks.

Self-assessment scale: Developing, Competent, Proficient. Benchmarks: Launch into Computing, 76 (October 2025); Understanding Artificial Intelligence, 87 (January 2026); Numerical Analysis, 99 (April 2026). A level change is claimed only where a module focused directly on that skill; elsewhere the improvement stayed within the same band, and the remaining gaps are what the upcoming modules and the dissertation should address.
